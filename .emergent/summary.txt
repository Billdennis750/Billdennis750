The user wants to build a professional website for a microfinance bank, Cashflow MFB. The initial goal was a simple loan application platform. This has evolved significantly to include a complex, multi-stage loan and repayment process.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
1.  **Core Functionality**: A platform for users to apply for collateral-free loans.
2.  **Complex Loan Flow**:
    *   A 5-step application form (Personal, Employment, Bank Details, Loan/Repayment Preferences, Identity & Account Creation).
    *   Applications are saved with a Payment Pending status before any fee is paid.
    *   A **₦2,500 processing fee** is required to move the application to Under Review.
    *   After approval, the user must pay a **₦3,000 fixed deposit** to proceed.
    *   The loan is disbursed 24 hours after the deposit is paid.
    *   The system must track multiple statuses: , , , , , .
3.  **User Authentication**: Users create their accounts during the loan application process and can log in with an email and password.
4.  **Payment Integration**: The system is integrated with **Xixapay** to handle the two separate payments (processing fee and fixed deposit) via a bank transfer/virtual account method.
5.  **User Dashboard**: A personal dashboard for users to track their detailed loan application status, see repayment information, and initiate pending payments. The UI has been enhanced for a professional fintech look.
6.  **Admin Dashboard**: A comprehensive dashboard for administrators to manage users, view all application details, monitor all payment transactions, and see platform statistics (total users, total paid, etc.).
7.  **Email Notifications**: Automated emails are sent for key events (e.g., application submission, approval, payment confirmation) using **SendGrid**. Emails are branded with the company logo.
8.  **Automated Reminders**: A background task sends email reminders every 24 hours to users who have pending payments.
9.  **Design & Branding**:
    *   The website uses the company's logo in the header and emails.
    *   A **Dark/Light mode** toggle is available on the homepage and dashboards.
    *   The Made with Emergent badge has been removed.
